# Animated Glass Background Config

- **enabled**: (true/false) Turn the glass effect on or off.
- **ambientGlowColor**: (CSS Color) The base color of the ambient glow.
- **ambientGlowOpacity**: (0.0 - 1.0) Opacity of the glow.
- **reflectionSpeed**: (number) Speed of the animated reflection line.
- **reflectionIntensity**: (0.0 - 1.0) Brightness of the reflection.
- **reflectionWidth**: (number) Width of the reflection line.
- **reflectionColor**: (CSS Color) Color of the reflection line.

Changing these settings requires a restart of Anki.
